# Sprints with a KPI check

Pick this when the team plans in fixed sprints and tracks a few numbers that say whether the work
is landing (stuck runs, error rate, review time).

- `cadences/sprint.md`: one page per two-week sprint, planned on its first Monday at 10:00. The
  people's page holds the plan (goals, committed issues, risks); each person's run records what
  they finished, carried over and picked up unplanned.
- `cadences/kpi.md`: a weekly check on Mondays at 11:00. Each person's run records the numbers they
  own and what changed them.

Set `sprint_start` to the first day of any of your sprints and `sprint_length` to its length
(`1w`, `2w`, `3w`). The schedule is the planning meeting's day and time; the page covers the whole
sprint. Most sprint teams also stand up daily: add `daily-standup` too.
